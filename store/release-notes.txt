ČHMÚ Weather 1.0.1

Public release of the unofficial ČHMÚ weather provider for Pebble.

- Excludes the authenticated ČHMÚ mobile-app service from the public build, including its domain permission and installation authentication
- Keeps public ČHMÚ station observations, ALADIN forecasts and optional Open-Meteo completion
- Uses Open-Meteo forecast UV by default, with an Off choice
- Prevents old saved settings or cached app data from re-enabling the excluded channels
- Updates English/Czech settings, source explanations and privacy information for the public build

ČHMÚ app daily summaries and clear-sky noon UV remain in a separately labelled personal build with a separate plugin UUID. It is not part of this public release or Store package.

Requires a Pebble mobile app supporting plugins. Coverage follows nearby Czech stations and forecast towns. Weather service availability and Pebble's import schedule control when new readings appear. No personal weather-service account, analytics or developer-operated server.

Validation results and remaining live-service/phone checks are recorded in the repository; publication eligibility is handled by the developer account.
