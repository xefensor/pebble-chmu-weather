ČHMÚ Weather 1.0.0

First stable release of the unofficial ČHMÚ weather provider for Pebble.

- Nearby ČHMÚ station temperature, humidity and wind with original measurement times
- ALADIN hourly forecasts and local daily ranges
- Independent ČHMÚ full-day forecast and clear-sky noon UV options
- Optional Open-Meteo completion for rain probabilities, missing fields and up to seven forecast days
- Pebble Weather, timeline and plugin-aware watchface integration
- English/Czech settings with light/dark appearance, station selection and expandable source details
- Reliable settings save errors and rollback to confirmed values
- Explicit forecast fallback labels, source timestamps and cache-age limits

Requires a Pebble mobile app supporting plugins. Coverage follows nearby Czech stations and forecast towns. Weather service availability and Pebble's import schedule control when new readings appear. No personal weather-service account, analytics or developer-operated server.

Validation results and remaining live-service/phone checks are recorded in the repository; publication eligibility is handled by the developer account.
