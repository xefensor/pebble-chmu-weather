ČHMÚ Weather 1.0.2

Publishing preparation for the public, phone-only weather plugin, including current permission descriptions. The public UUID, provider domains, units and original observation/forecast times remain unchanged.

Choose nearby or fixed Czech stations, local ALADIN forecasts and optional Open-Meteo completion in English or Czech settings. No personal backend or account is used by the public plugin.

This is a weather source, separate from Activities such as Timer countdowns. There is no watch app to open.
