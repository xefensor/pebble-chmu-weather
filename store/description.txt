Czech station observations and local ALADIN forecasts for your Pebble.

ČHMÚ Weather is an unofficial weather plugin that runs inside the Pebble phone app. Choose it as your weather provider for Pebble Weather, the timeline and compatible readings in Dynamic, Halftone, Dashboard and other apps.

- Use your saved Pebble locations and units.
- Find a nearby station automatically or choose a fixed Czech station.
- See station temperature, with humidity and wind when available.
- Get hourly and daily ALADIN forecasts.
- Add missing forecast fields, rain probabilities, forecast UV and later days through Open-Meteo completion. It is enabled by default and can be turned off in settings.
- Configure the plugin in English or Czech.

Covers Czech locations. Nearby station observations may differ from conditions at your exact location. Cached readings keep their original age; Pebble controls native Weather update timing.

Requires internet access and plugin support enabled in the official Pebble phone app. This is a phone plugin with no watch app to open.

Free. Part of XOCE. No additional account, API key, ads, analytics or developer-operated server. Requests go to ČHMÚ and, when enabled, Open-Meteo. Independent of ČHMÚ, Open-Meteo and Core Devices.

Source and privacy: https://github.com/xefensor/pebble-chmu-weather
