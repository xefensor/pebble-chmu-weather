Unofficial ČHMÚ weather for Pebble, focused on nearby Czech station measurements and local ALADIN forecasts.

Fresh station readings supply current temperature, humidity and wind when reported together. Public ALADIN forecasts fill unavailable fields. Optional Open-Meteo adds rain probabilities, missing fields, forecast UV and forecasts up to seven days.

Select ČHMÚ Weather as Pebble's Weather provider to feed the watch's Weather app, timeline and compatible watchfaces. Plugin-aware apps can also read the standard weather/location source directly. Saved locations and units follow Pebble.

Features:
- Automatic nearby station selection or a fixed station
- Original observation times and clear measured/forecast status
- Hourly and daily forecasts with source information
- Open-Meteo completion and a separate forecast UV on/off choice
- Calculated feels-like temperature and sunrise/sunset
- Local caching without making old observations look fresh
- English/Czech settings, light/dark themes and readable diagnostics

Czech coverage only; nearby stations do not measure your exact street. UV is a forecast, not a live measurement. Pebble controls native Weather update timing.

Requires a preview-enabled Pebble mobile app. Uses public ČHMÚ website/open-data products and optional Open-Meteo; the public release does not access ČHMÚ's authenticated mobile-app service. No personal account, ads, analytics or developer-operated server. Independent of ČHMÚ, Open-Meteo and Core Devices.

Source and privacy: https://github.com/xefensor/pebble-chmu-weather

ČHMÚ Weather supplies weather readings for Pebble Weather and compatible apps. Choose its readings separately from Activities. An activity is an ongoing update, such as a Timer countdown shown on Dynamic or Halftone.
